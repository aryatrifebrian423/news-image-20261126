## Method

- Primary anchors: the 26 November 2026 morning roundups (Jagran
  Josh school-assembly headlines, Observer Voice, SelfStudys,
  pw.live, Libre News of the World) cross-checked against wire and
  outlet reporting (Reuters, AP via local papers, CNN, Xinhua, ESA,
  Nature, SpacePolicyOnline).
- Two sources per story where possible; single-source items are the
  roundup reports themselves, which aggregate the wires.
- Where an event itself happened overnight on 25 November, the story
  carried is the 26 November development: the confirmed toll, the
  market reaction, the official response. The day's own anchors —
  Thanksgiving Day (USA), India's Constitution Day, the 18th
  anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai attacks, the World Chess
  Championship Game 3, the Palm Beach ferry strike — are all 26
  November events.
